FAQ
What is Curo?

Curo is a healthcare web application designed to help people review written care instructions and organise follow-up tasks.

Does ClearCare provide medical advice?

No. ClearCare organises wording from the source as a reading aid. AI-assisted explanations can be imperfect; always compare the result with the original instructions and ask a qualified healthcare professional about anything unclear or conflicting.

Can I save my care tasks and appointments?

Yes. After creating an account and signing in, you can manage tasks, appointments and medication records in your personal dashboard.
